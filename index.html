Basyo Memo · iPhone

Where are you?

A to-do app that keeps each task in the place where you'll actually do it. Pick a place, open its floor plan, tap a room — and only see what's waiting there.

Download on the App Store Free · No sign-up · Works offline
Concept

Put your to-dos where they happen.

Most to-do apps line everything up in one long list. Basyo Memo puts each task in the place where you'll do it — so the kitchen only shows kitchen things.

A usual to-do app

  • Clean the sink
  • Print the report
  • Change the bed sheets

  • Reply to the professor
  • Water the plants
  • Check the detergent
  • Return library books
  • …

Basyo Memo — try it

HOME
Kitchen

    All clear. Nothing waiting here.

    Tap a room in the floor plan. When you finish a task, it disappears from the room — and the room gets tidier.
    The dots on each room are unfinished to-dos — you can see where it's messiest at a glance.
    How it works

    Place → Space → To-do

    Three layers, the same way you move through your day.

    01

    Place

    Choose where you are — home, school, work.

    02

    Space

    Its floor plan appears. Tap the kitchen to see only the kitchen.

    03

    To-do

    Finish a task and it leaves the room. The space gets cleaner with it.

    We're two students making this in our spare time, so we can't promise to reply to every message — but we read every bug report.

    It helps to include your iOS version, iPhone model, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

    Where is my data stored?

    Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and the app works offline.

    I reinstalled the app and my tasks are gone.

    Deleting the app also deletes its data. Reinstalling cannot bring it back.

    How many places can I create?

    Three for free, with unlimited rooms and tasks. Pro removes the limit on places with a single purchase.

    I bought Pro but a new iPhone does not have it.

    Open the Pro screen and tap “Restore purchase” while signed in with the same Apple Account.
